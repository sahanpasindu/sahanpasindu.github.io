Simple tools for quick video clips

SplitClip focuses on the common actions you need before sharing a video: split, trim, export, save, and share.

Split by duration or quantity

Cut longer videos into equal parts by time or by number of clips, useful for status videos and quick sharing.

Custom split points

Choose your own split points when you need more control over where each video clip starts and ends.

Trim and cut

Remove unwanted parts and keep only the section you want before exporting your final clip.

Social-ready clips

Save and share exported clips through your favorite apps using your device’s normal sharing options.

Local video processing

During normal app use, selected videos are processed locally on your device. SplitClip does not upload your selected videos to our own server for splitting, trimming, or exporting.
